How I improve campaign performance

A loop, run for every client
01 · Monitor

Watch every campaign against target

A pacing agent I built checks 100+ live campaigns against media plan targets every morning, two hours before the working day starts.

For exampleAn overspending Google campaign or a Meta campaign behind its lead target is flagged before anyone opens Ads Manager.
02 · Diagnose

Find where budget is wasted

Break results down by platform, audience, placement, creative, keyword and market to locate gaps and quick wins.

For exampleSearch leads at a fifth of the cost of awareness leads for the same audience.
03 · Optimise

Recommend the change and its impact

Budget shifts, audience and creative changes, objective and bidding changes, each with the expected effect on leads and cost.

For exampleMove a share of the weakest segment's budget to the strongest, in steps, and track cost per lead.
04 · Communicate

Get it actioned

Present findings in client meetings and to internal strategy, performance and customer success teams. Pacing flags reach both teams by email every morning.

For exampleMonthly reviews and end-of-campaign reports that open with decisions, not charts.

    How it's built
    1. Windsor.ai pulls Meta and Google Ads daily into one BigQuery table, with platform naming mapped to shared columns.
    2. Targets live in a separate sheet, joined by campaign and date, so pacing compares actuals with the expected share of target to date.
    3. The KPI selector is one Looker Studio parameter; a CASE field swaps the metric for every chart at once.
    4. Cost per result is calculated as SUM(spend) / SUM(results), never an average of daily ratios.
    5. Arabic and English search terms are kept as written, since both drive leads in GCC markets.

    Nissan · Automotive · Qatar · Forecasting

    Forecasting leads before a campaign launches

    Budget splits for new launches were set from last month's spreadsheet. The client wanted to know how many leads a planned campaign would bring, and at what cost, before spending anything.

    0.72
    R² on campaigns the model never saw
    47K
    daily platform rows modelled
    How it works
    DataA year of daily data across Meta, Google, Snapchat, TikTok and X, grouped to campaign and month to remove noise.
    ModelLinear, Ridge and Random Forest compared in scikit-learn. Inputs match a campaign brief: platform, objective, car model, month and spend.
    UseForecasts leads and cost per lead per platform and month for new briefs, such as a van launch and an aftersales campaign, so budget scenarios are compared before launch.
    LimitsOne-off seasonal pushes like Back to School are hardest to predict. The next version adds car-model cost history.
